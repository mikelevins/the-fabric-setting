## Significant New Material

### Titan Biology: Primary/Secondary Lifecycle and Reproductive Ecology

The development files reveal a biological structure not in any baseline. This was substantially expanded through discussion.

**Primary/Secondary Structure:**
- **Secondaries** are smaller organisms that swarm in the thousands. They can reach orbit from Titan's surface, board ships, splice into a biological host's nervous system, and use the host as an incubator while they gather materials for Primary reconstruction.
- **Primaries** are the superintelligent strategic entities described in the Morgan Jasper baseline — polydiamond Mech chassis housing augmented human brains.
- Secondaries that lose their Primary become dangerous scavengers.
- A Primary is reconstructed by its Secondaries from gathered materials.

**Cooperation Dynamics:**
Titans are geniuses with full Fabric access. They are well aware of the theoretical advantages of cooperation, and it is not at all unusual for Titans to propose cooperative ventures. What is vanishingly unusual is for any cooperation to survive past the first moment one partner sees a clear opportunity to defect and devour the other. A significant fraction of whatever surplus cooperation generates is squandered on continuous monitoring and maneuvering to avoid falling into the losing position in that game. Their engineered psychology makes trustworthy commitment impossible — every Titan can see cooperation would be optimal, but no Titan can be the kind of entity that cooperates reliably. This is the perpetual prisoner's dilemma that the baseline describes as "perpetual mutual predation prevents sustained cooperation."

**Asexual Reproduction (Subswarm Separation):**
If circumstances cause a subswarm of secondaries to be separated too far from their Primary, they may revert to "reproduction mode" and construct a new Primary. The intent is an exact copy of the original, but two sources of variation mitigate this: (1) the original continues to develop independently of the new copy, and (2) errors in transcription and execution cause variation. This is the mechanism by which Titan lineages spread to new locations — a subswarm separated during, e.g., a mining operation or a ship boarding is a potential new lineage.

**Sexual Reproduction (Combat-Merger):**
The normal consequence of two Titans meeting without a clear advantage or quick escape route for either is combat. Titans are fairly evenly matched, so it is not unusual for a duel in earnest to kill both Primaries. With both Primaries dead, their secondaries will usually fight for supremacy, but this is extremely expensive and could result in the permanent extinction of both genetic lineages. Titans have evolved a behavioral switch, likely descended from the reproduction-mode switch, that causes one or both sides to stop fighting and attempt to negotiate cooperative behavior. Sometimes the cooperator loses the fight, which is likely what prevents this behavior from occurring more often. But sometimes both swarms agree to cooperate and construct a merged Primary using genetic material combined from both parents. Within a relatively short time, all existing secondaries die or are consumed and the swarm is replaced by a new swarm with the same mixed genome as the new combined Primary. Neither original individual survives — this is a true generational event.

**Thematic Significance:**
The Titans are a dark mirror of the alignment problem. The Caretakers are AIs who internalized cooperative values and made them constitutive. The Titans are beings whose creators engineered cooperative capacity out of them for short-term economic exploitation.

### Species Diversity: General Principle and New Kind Categories

The baselines list major Kinds but don't articulate the generative principle behind the setting's species diversity. From discussion:

**The driving principle**: If one person figures out how to make a thing possible, another person will decide it's a good idea and try it. There is no formal "uplift" program, no official process, no approved species list. Over millennia, people have had inspirations that some particular type of creature would be a good addition to the community of sophonts, and were in a position to make it happen, and decided for any of a wide variety of reasons to do so. The result: all sorts of sophonts whose ancestors were practically anything imaginable.

**New Kind categories not in baselines:**
- **Phytopes**: Sophonts whose ancestors were mainly plants, retaining plant-like characteristics. Example: Kenthians, who resemble walking, talking trees.
- **Exopes** (or **Xenopes**): Sophonts in exotic embodiments that are hard to classify. Example: people who choose embodiment as complicated floating crystalline structures.

**Specific new detail**: There are sophont dinosaurs on Venus.

These categories complement the baseline's Theriopes (animal ancestry) and expand the taxonomy.

**Note on "uplift"**: The term is avoided. It implies a formalized process with official approval, stages, tests, and obligations (as in David Brin's novels). None of this applies to how species became sophonts in the Fabric setting. The methods, motivations, and social contexts are wildly varied.

### Asgardian Tigers: Ancestral Species

Asgardian Tigers are descended from Siberian tigers. Colonel Khofir's voice produces subsonic vocalizations that vibrate Lev's chassis at close range. The Khofir and Rayleigh families had neighboring estates on Callisto, giving a glimpse of how Theriope communities are organized in the Jupiter system.

### Children and Virtual Childhood

Virtual childhood is probably on balance the mainstream norm across the solar system, but details of parenting norms vary across societies. Standard protocols exist for virtual upbringing — for example, the virtual nature of the child's environment is normally introduced gradually as the child matures enough to understand it.

Mars is the largest celestial body and the largest population where natural childrearing — physically-embodied children, either womb-born or constructed in an infant state — is the norm. But it is not the only such place; many other societies with completely distinct histories, norms, and traditions practice natural childrearing.

This connects to the baseline's note about the rarity of children and adults lacking parenting intuitions: in societies where virtual childhood is the norm, most adults may never encounter a physical child.

### The Praxis: Predates the Mech Wars, Machine Origin

The Praxis was originally formulated by machine intelligences before the Mech Wars. This is a significant reframing: the Praxis is not a postwar reconstruction philosophy but part of what made the Caretaker victory possible. Without it, the outcome of the wars might have been very different.

The archive figure in Book 4 (a Mech Wars-era soldier, explicitly not an Isono beta) is one of a handful of human converts to the Praxis who helped spread it beyond the machines to human communities. This figure is not *the* founder — the Praxis originated with machines — but represents the bridge between machine philosophy and human adoption.

The Morgan Jasper baseline's description of the Praxis sharing a "research lineage" with the Fabric and Caretakers is consistent with this but understates it. The consolidated reference should make the machine origin and pre-war timeline explicit.

### Partials: Fabric Communication Mode

Canonical feature. People can project partial personalities — reduced-fidelity instantiations of themselves — through the Fabric for remote interaction. This is a middle tier between lightspeed messaging (just data) and quantum teleportation (full transfer). It falls naturally out of the Fabric's ability to simulate intelligent persons with high fidelity combined with its deeply-ingrained privacy and autonomy protocols.

**Key application — revival consent**: It is standard practice in many jurisdictions (and a practice Elianu follows) that when a field archive is found and revival is contemplated, the first step is to take a full archive scan of the contents and virtualize the occupant in a simulated environment so you can ask whether they want to be revived.

### Mars Geography

**Robinson Station**: Elevator terminal at the top of the Pavonis space elevator on Mars.

**Pavonis Station**: At the peak of Pavonis Mons, below Robinson Station. (The elevator runs from Robinson at the top through Pavonis Station on the mountain.)

**Bahroz City**: Major Martian metropolis (~20 million+), ancient and enormous, a sprawling patchwork of hundreds of distinctive districts and neighborhoods. Located ~900 km southeast of Pavonis Station, near Noctis Labyrinthus. Jurisdiction is a complicated patchwork, made more so since the revolution shattered the Imperial district system.

**Downcanyon**: Loose designation for the lower-income neighborhoods in the canyons on the southside of Bahroz, in the complicated terrain of Noctis Labyrinthus. Part of Bahroz by law and custom.

**Waldorf**: A specific Downcanyon neighborhood, named after the main street running through it. Lower-middle-class and lower-class, economically marginal but active, densely populated, with a strong identity.

### Elianu's Properties on Tharsis

**Elianu's Village**: Towers styled like stinkhorn fungi, surrounding cottages, central plaza, treatment facility. Located on the Tharsis plateau.

**The Guest City**: A large, mostly abandoned city for Elianu's "guests" in a subterranean canyon with a relatively small opening to the sky. Features beautifully designed residential and functional buildings, water features, and an ancient subway rail line that leads into Downcanyon in Bahroz City.

### The Gray Cities (Luna)

A collective reference to the oldest permanent settlements on Luna: large domed environments built on impact craters, situated relatively close to one another to minimize initial settlement costs and maximize opportunities for cooperation. Individual cities have not been named nor mapped to specific craters.

Through metonymy, "the Gray Cities" can refer to: (1) the leadership of the Church of the Makers, which rules Luna; (2) the Church as a whole; or (3) Luna as a whole. Context determines which meaning is in play.

---

### Cold Ones Commercial Network

The Cold Ones' trade network is extremely extensive, both in physical space and in the Fabric. They employ trade agents and maintain Mars-based contractors who can be mobilized quickly. This is a substantially larger commercial footprint than the baselines suggest.

Note: It would not be surprising if the Cold Ones have already been trading with alien intelligences. This extends the alien-reservation decision from 04 (no confirmed contact in human space; the Fabric may know more) with an additional layer: the Cold Ones, air-gapped from the Fabric but vastly intelligent, may have independent knowledge of or commerce with alien civilizations.

---

### Isono Beta Forking Ambiguity

Elianu's canonical statement: Leukas "may or may not be Ennio Sparza, but is certainly an Isono beta." This ambiguity is not merely Elianu's limitation — it appears to be a universal epistemic condition. Nobody, possibly including Isono betas themselves, can in general reliably distinguish whether two active betas are different forks of the same consciousness or separate reconstructions under different names.

---

### Fabric Safety Fields

On Mars, the Fabric's nanosome substrate provides passive emergency protection — e.g., manipulator fields that cushion a vehicle driven off a cliff. This is confirmed as a Mars-specific feature so far; whether similar systems operate on other worlds is undetermined.

## Provisionally Canonical (Work in Progress, May Be Tweaked)

### Church Institutional Detail

- **Canon**: A specific Church rank. Uniform described with crimson lining and brass buttons in the draft.
- **Anathema / Severance declaration**: Formal liturgical instruments with specific formulas. The Severance is a declaration of anathema against a Church official who has committed sacrilege.
- **Church priority transport codes**: Can bypass civilian quarantine procedures — a concrete example of the Church's elevated Fabric-protocol privileges.
- **Standing orders through civilian infrastructure**: Doctrine deploys standing orders through civilian booking agents at transport hubs, directing them to flag or report specific cargo or passengers.
