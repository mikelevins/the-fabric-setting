# The Ark Program (~2900–3000 AD)

**Source**: mikel, 2026-09-29 (Book 4 sitting; ruled while working out Oleh Itzal and Angela Cygni's home society). Ruled canon; derived items marked.
**Scope**: system-wide.

---

## Where and who

- **The Belt built the arks, inside a coalition program** funded from all the richest worlds.
- **The premise:** after the Mech Wars, enough time had passed that human population was recovering and a real civilization was growing and developing, **with low-key help from the Caretakers**; the most prosperous and forward-looking societies decided they were prosperous enough to organize and fund interstellar exploration. Arks: ~40 km orbital cities with huge torches, vast stored resources, their own fabs and resource-gathering affordances, populations in the thousands; the *Angel of Cygnus* and other arks bound for other stars (dates: `09`).

## How it grew

1. **A few forward-looking visionaries** build up a body of public commentary.
2. **A grassroots swell of support** feeds an optimistic vision of the future.
3. It becomes **a broad movement whose participants see the ark project as the triumph of an optimistic vision of human progress over a dark history.**
4. The movement **recruits support from different worlds and social coalitions.**
5. It culminates in **the Belt-based construction effort, funded by contributors on all the richest worlds, with contributions funneled through Ceres, Mars, Earth, and Ganymede** — **creating huge fortunes that later go to fund large-scale colonization and terraforming efforts across the Solar System.**

## Infiltration

- **Several of the arks were infiltrated by Abjurers — maybe all of them**, which might mean that none succeeded in its mission; **not yet known for sure.** The *Angel of Cygnus* is the known case: a legitimate ark and an Abjurer long con at once (`04`, `07`).

## Ship minds

- Built by people who thought of ship minds as property; operated mostly by people who treated them as people (`11`).
