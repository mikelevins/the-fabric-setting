## All Decisions Made

1. **Canonical authority**: Morgan Jasper is the default authority. Kestrel references take precedence only case-by-case where novel-published canon is at stake.
2. **Political terminology**: "Jovian Confederation" = formal name. "League" = colloquial. "Consortium" = specifically the Jovian Defense Consortium.
3. **MMORPG visuals**: Game-design abstraction, not literal Fabric canon. Visual touchstones only.
4. **Diplomatic Guard**: An organ of the JDC. The JDC is the Confederation's executive arm for external affairs (defense, diplomacy, law enforcement, intelligence). Ambassadors are Consortium officials; treaties require dual ratification by Consortium and Lands and Houses.
5. **Generation ship timeline**: Loose. ~5,000 years for wars, ~4,000 for generation ships. Firmer dates if a story needs them.
6. **Volvox and Adlivun**: Adopted as canonical Fabric-infrastructure place names.
7. **Angel origins**: Isono's suggestion. Caretakers model real human personalities. Standard function is diplomatic liaison, not emergency response. Caretaker restraint has increased over millennia. Angel spec has drifted as "human" has expanded.
8. **Second Imperium**: Mars-based military dictatorship. Fell ~70 years before Book 3. Further development deferred.
9. **Far-future canon**: Included as loosely-held canonical trajectory. Story wins over main-era rules if needed.
10. **Kinship vs. indifference**: Stated explicitly. Three layers: alignment-research origin, kinship vs. indifference axis, indifference becomes evil at scale.
11. **Park ranger terminology**: Loose umbrella term. Mandate is population-level ecological management, not individual intervention.
12. **Mech Wars dating**: Acute phase ~2400–2500 AD, lasting weeks. "Reversals in the twenty-eighth and twenty-ninth centuries" (Book 2) = late Abjurer cleanup. "Circa 2300 AD / Prebellum" (Book 3) = correct, prebellum. Story present ~7005 AD. Post-war history ~4,500 years (often rounded to ~5,000). No published text requires retcon; the "late 22nd century" working assumption is retired. New worldbuilding: three-timescale war, physical substrate dependency, distribution principle, protocol-level exploit threat. Full resolution in `09-mech-wars-dating-resolution.md`.

## Structural Decisions Made

1. **Single doc vs. multiple files?** Multi-file, following the Morgan Jasper pattern. One topic per file, loadable independently.

2. **What goes in vs. stays out?** Character-specific material stays in home projects. The consolidated reference covers: cosmology, physics, the Fabric, species/Kinds, technology, regions/astrography, institutions, history, the Praxis, social-variation framework, and the deep-future era (loosely held).

3. **Canonical authority**: Morgan Jasper is the default authority. ✓

4. **Versioning**: Consolidated reference is the authority; projects that develop new worldbuilding should push changes back to the consolidated doc.

---

## Proposed Consolidated Reference Structure

1. **Core Premises** — Timeline (~7005 AD present; wars ~2400–2500 AD; ~4,500 years post-war), no aliens (refined: none encountered in human space; Fabric may know more), real physics + Fabric exception, solar system scope, social diversity.
2. **The Fabric** — Infrastructure, self-governance, digital civilizations, communications, time multiplier (~10⁶), manufacturing, Translation. Volvox and Adlivun as named infrastructure nodes. Physical substrate dependency. Fundamental protocols below faction level.
3. **The Mech Wars and Faction History** — Prebellum era (~4 centuries of development), alignment-research origins, kinship vs. indifference, the three factions, the acute war (three timescales), physical substrate damage, the reconstruction, centuries of Abjurer cleanup, the distribution principle.
4. **Species and Kinds** — The extinction reset, Kind bias, species permeability, rarity of children, the diversity principle. All major Kinds with descriptions. Model lineages (Leslie, Hama) as examples. Phytopes, Exopes.
5. **Core Technologies** — Reconstruction/creche, re-embodiment, torch ships, elevators, packet drones, quantum teleportation, Fabric manufacturing, military tech, environment envelopes (gold radiation shielding), gasherd membranes, manipulators, partials.
6. **The Caretaker System** — Angel origins, waldos, park rangers, the restraint arc, Angel specification drift. Park-ranger mandate. Structural necessity of protecting physical civilization (substrate dependency).
7. **Major Regions** — Inner system, Belt (the Golden Way, Belt confederation, Golden Way Academy), Jupiter system (Confederation, three-legged institutional structure, kritarchies, dreadnoughts, Callisto), Saturn system, outer system. Mars with post-Imperium landscape, Participant zones, Elianu.
8. **Key Institutions** — Church of the Makers (theology, Fabric-protocol privileges, Office of Doctrine, the Host, the Primate, internal politics, Isono beta infiltration), the Praxis (machine origin, predates wars), the Participants (jobs urge, Church entanglement), Lands and Houses, sureties (private-sector), Institutes of Research. Second Imperium (brief historical note).
9. **Deep History and Key Figures** — Kenjiro Isono and the betas (the archive threat as protocol-level exploit), Elianu and Church co-founding, Auron II and the Second Imperium, Ilona Biru and the Instrument of Confederation.
10. **Dimensions of Social Variation** — The open-ended framework of questions. Regional phenotype clusters. "Reds" and "Elves."
11. **Deep Future** (appendix, loosely-held) — Far-future era outcomes, interstellar probes as distribution-principle endpoint, Mars re-colonization.
12. **Open Questions** — Areas acknowledged as undeveloped. Including: prebellum history detail, war trigger, Belt confederation name and development, Jovian institutional history, Ilona Biru, arbeiter-to-person transition.
