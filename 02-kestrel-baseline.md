## Game Layer Material: The Fabric from the Inside (NON-CANONICAL)

**DECIDED**: The MMORPG's geometric visual language is a game-design abstraction, not literal Fabric canon. The material below is preserved for reference as visual touchstones, but the specific mechanics (cube grids, equipment slots, name-generation integers, Platonic solid architecture) are not setting canon. The consolidated reference will describe Fabric-native civilizations using the Morgan Jasper baseline's framework (millions of societies, digital civilizations, self-governance) without importing the game's specific visual system.

### Fabric-Native Visual Identity (Game Only)
- Fabric inhabitants represented as 4×4×4 cube grids (64 cubes total) with faction-based color schemes: Caretakers (green/growth), Abjurers (red-black/crystalline), Rogues (blue-white/adaptive).
- Platonic solid architectural language scaling from personal to system-spanning megastructures.
- 64-bit integer name generation system with eight semantic domains.

### Locations Referenced (Pending Decision)
- **Volvox** (at Jupiter): Major computational hub. Name originates in the game but may be adopted as canonical independently.
- **Adlivun** (at Sedna): Deep-space monitoring relay. Same status as Volvox.

## New Material: Published Novel Canon (Books 1–3)

### Character Designations and Species Detail
The novels establish full designations and model information not in the Morgan Jasper baseline:
- **Lev**: Ixion Eleven Chrysotile. Leslie Model 11 Mech. Cyan-tinted metal carapace. Manufactured at Ceres, trained on Mars. Former Jovian Diplomatic Guard medic.
- **Verge**: 17 Actinium Converges. Hama Model 17 Ionian. Chrome egg-shaped body. The "Hama" designation is novel-specific — the Morgan Jasper baseline uses "Ionian" generically.
- **Mai Greenhill**: Canine (Spearhound from South Asgard Preserve, Callisto). Grey/tan coat, pointed ears. Exceptional hearing. Master of manipulator field technology.
- **Yarrow**: Ionian. Golden skin, white-gold crest, sky-blue eyes. Jasper's sibling (confirmed connective tissue between novels and Jasper stories, consistent with baseline).
- **Kestrel** (ship AI): Described as "Rayleigh Shipping's oldest working vessel." Mature, polite, extremely competent personality.

### The Zang/Erdos Clone-Displacement Thread
The novels develop a specific case study in reconstruction ethics:
- **Erszbet Zang**: The original person, formerly Solomon Security. Died. Her brain archive was used to reconstruct **Erszbet Erdos**, who took over Zang's former life, career, family relationships, and social position.
- Zang was later reconstructed separately, finding herself displaced from her own identity. She carries a grandmother's flamethrower, works as able spacer on Kestrel.
- Erdos is Solomon's Security Chief, daughter of Captain-General Erdos (adopted the family name and rank).
- This thread concretizes the Morgan Jasper baseline's discussion of reconstruction raising "identity questions" and the note that "legal status of post-transfer persons varies by jurisdiction."

### Angel of Cygnus (Book 2)
A generation ship launched approximately 4,000 years before story time during post-Mech-Wars reconstruction. It was secretly constructed as an Abjurer "ark" — carrying an ancient Abjurer AI hidden in its systems, along with thousands of biological and cybernetic weapons designed to seed the solar system with viruses to exterminate biological life. The enemy AI killed the crew over the ship's 30-year subjective journey (relativistic time dilation). The ship mind ("Angel") was tortured and fragmented for decades. The Kestrel crew discovered and rescued the sole survivor (Oleh Itzal, a systems engineer), confronted the Abjurer AI, and freed Angel's consciousness. Angel was reconstructed in a new mechanical body. The ancient enemy was left trapped alone within the Cold Ones-encased hull.

This adds to the baseline's sparse treatment of Abjurer threats with a concrete example of long-dormant Abjurer operations. The mention of other possible "arks" implies this isn't an isolated case.

### Auron the Second / Timur Serik (Book 3)
Ennio Sparza, the last emperor of the Second Imperium (a fallen empire referenced in historical context), operated for decades under the false identity of Timur Serik, a Solomon Security lieutenant. He orchestrated the theft of a field archive containing a ~4,500-year-old preserved soldier (possibly Kenjiro Isono) through a conspiracy involving Church of the Makers personnel and corrupted Solomon officials. The Book 4 development notes reveal he is actually the first Isono beta, reconstructed before the Mech Wars, and is obsessively seeking the final Isono field archive because he believes it contains Fabric-subversion protocols.

### Solomon (Dreadnought)
Described as a spherical ship 10 kilometers in diameter with internal gravity from rotation, transit tubes, automated cars, artificial day/night cycles, and internal cities. This is consistent with the Morgan Jasper baseline's description of dreadnoughts as "small-dwarf-planet scale" simultaneously functioning as military bases, cities, economic hubs, and mobile jurisdictions.

### The Cold Ones (Novel Appearances)
Books 1–2 give the Cold Ones concrete story presence: they hire the Kestrel crew for the Angel of Cygnus rescue mission (Book 2). Communication occurs through "consciousness expansion" — the Cold Ones interface with individuals through the Fabric. They encase the Angel of Cygnus hull for study after the rescue. Their "perfect credit rating" is used as a character detail rather than just a worldbuilding note.

### Political Terminology (RESOLVED)
The novels use "Jovian League" and "Consortium" where the Morgan Jasper baseline uses "Jovian Confederation." These have been reconciled: "Jovian Confederation" is the formal name for the political entity. "League" is a commonly-used informal synonym. "Consortium" refers specifically to the Jovian Defense Consortium (JDC).

### The Diplomatic Guard (RESOLVED)
The novels reference the "Jovian Diplomatic Guard" with divisions including Criminal Investigation Unit (CIU) and Strategic Sciences. The Diplomatic Guard is an organ of the Jovian Defense Consortium. The JDC is not merely a military — it is the Confederation's executive arm for all external affairs: defense, diplomacy, law enforcement, and intelligence. The Diplomatic Guard is its service branch handling law enforcement, intelligence, and diplomatic representation. Jovian Ambassadors are Consortium officials; they negotiate agreements requiring ratification by both the Consortium and the Lands and Houses. The Jovian Consular Estate on Mars, for example, is governed by the Jovian Ambassador on behalf of Jovian society.

## New Material: Book 4 Development Notes

The Book 4 development discussion (October 2025) contains revelations flagged as available for the fourth novel. Many of these are already reflected in the Morgan Jasper baseline (which was created more recently and incorporated them), but some add specificity:

### Titan Origins (Explicit)
"Genetically-modified human brains in alien bodies, created by development company to exploit Titan resources. Modifications created ruthless cannibal psychopaths prevented from conquest only by mutual betrayal tendencies." This is consistent with the Morgan Jasper baseline's treatment but stated more bluntly — the baseline describes "polydiamond Mech chassis housing augmented human brains" with "ruthless sociopathy" and "perpetual mutual predation."

### Isono Beta Network (More Detail)
Book 4 notes describe the beta network with more operational specificity than the baseline:
- Auron II (Timur Serik) is the first Isono beta, reconstructed before the Mech Wars.
- He believes the final field archive contains Fabric-subversion protocols ("godlike powers over the foundation of civilization").
- Canon Leukas: Head of the Office of Doctrine (Church secret police), another Isono beta. Leukas has a personal ancient hatred of Elianu and an extensive spy network monitoring all unusual Elianu traffic.
- The betas are described as "hundreds of Isono betas operating as loose secret society infiltrating institutions" — consistent with the baseline's 60–120 active population estimate and mycelium-network organizational ecology.
- The "Walter White dynamic" is explicitly named: betas start as decent people and devolve depending on circumstances.
- Angel Keryx is confirmed as a successful Isono reconstruction by Elianu — "benevolent Isono beta."

### Elianu (More Personal Detail)
- "Brilliant geneticist who reconstructed first Isono beta" — adds scientific specialty.
- "Co-created Church of the Makers with reconstructed Isono, disagreeing over its cynical nature" — the baseline describes the Church founding more clinically. This adds interpersonal dimension: Elianu and Isono disagreed about the Church's cynicism from the start.
- "Experiencing crisis of faith after millennia" — flagged as a Book 4 theme.
- "Atonement vocation" — reconstructing Isono betas carefully, trying to do better.
- The Book 4 notes suggest a potential internal-conflict focus: "intrapersonal rather than interpersonal conflict within Elianu."

### Yaug Revealed
Book 4 confirms Yaug as "waldo operated by Caretaker entities serving as 'park rangers' for physical universe wildlife. Has own consciousness but also serves as remote instrument. Millions of such waldos exist throughout solar system." This was revealed to Esgar and Jaemon off-stage after Book 3. Consistent with baseline.

### The Praxis (Earlier Mention)
The Book 4 notes describe the Praxis as "ancient machine religion/ethical system based on mathematical optimization" and a "Pareto optimization" system. The Morgan Jasper baseline's more developed treatment describes it as a "practical, goal-oriented ethical discipline based on iterative Pareto improvements" with sanctuary traditions and self-correcting honesty. The baseline is more detailed; the Book 4 note represents an earlier, less refined formulation.

### Fabric Scale Revelation
"Millions of complete societies exist in the Fabric, orders of magnitude larger than physical civilization. Most Fabric inhabitants unaware physical society exists." This is directly reflected in the Morgan Jasper baseline. Consistent.

## Remaining Discrepancies

### Mech Model Designations
The novels give specific model lineages: "Leslie Model 11" (Lev), "Hama Model 17" (Verge as Ionian). The Morgan Jasper baseline discusses model lineages generally ("function somewhat like ethnicity") but doesn't use these specific names. No conflict — the novel designations add specificity.

### Church of the Makers — Novel vs. Baseline
The novels and Book 4 notes give the Church more operational presence than the baseline: Church offices aboard Defense Consortium dreadnoughts, the Sacred Host operating as military police, chaplains holding secular positions (Gebre Isaac as customs chief). The baseline provides the theological and historical framework. No conflict — complementary.

### Generation Ships (Pending Decision)
Book 2 establishes generation ships as launched "4,000+ years ago during post-war reconstruction." The approximate timeline works if the ship launched roughly 1,000 years after the Mech Wars, during the reconstruction/Church-founding era. See `04-conflicts-and-decisions.md` Item 5.
