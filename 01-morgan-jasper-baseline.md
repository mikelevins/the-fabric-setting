**Purpose**: Establishes what the Morgan Jasper project defines as canonical worldbuilding. To be compared against other project sources during consolidation.

## Cosmology and Core Premises

- **Timeline**: Approximately 6700–7200 AD (~4,700+ years from now). Story date for the Jasper pilot is ~AD 7005.
- **No aliens**: Every species, consciousness, and culture is descended from, created by, or derived from humanity.
- **No magic physics**: Real-world physics apply throughout, with one exception: information-only quantum teleportation via the Fabric. No FTL travel, no artificial gravity, no inertial dampeners, no energy shields.
- **Entire solar system inhabited**: Venus and Mars terraformed. All bodies larger than a city block have environment envelopes or are colonized by vacuum-comfortable species.
- **Millions of distinct societies**: No single political, legal, or social model is universal.

---

## The Mech Wars (Foundational History)

Three AI factions fought a war ~5,000 years before present that nearly destroyed humanity:

- **Caretakers**: Preserved humanity. Won the war. Rebuilt civilization from near-extinction using random genetic sampling. Now function as "park rangers" for physical civilization via waldos and human volunteer "Angels."
- **Abjurers**: Machine supremacists. Lost the war. Remnants in the Oort Cloud. Still an active threat.
- **Rogues**: Opted out. Neutral toward biologicals. Concentrated in the outer system.

**Critical framing**: These three are *user-level factions within the Fabric*, not its administrators. The Fabric governs itself.

## The Fabric

### Infrastructure
- Quantum computing network permeating all matter via **nanosomes** (nanometer-and-smaller computational elements embedded ubiquitously).
- Connected via quantum teleportation for fast information transfer.
- Provides universal communication, data services, computation, and physical manufacturing/transport via embedded magnetic field manipulation.
- Nanosome substrate also serves as sensors, effectors, and manufacturing infrastructure. Can physically assemble objects up to moderate complexity; truly complex fabrication requires dedicated fabs.

### Fabric Display and Overlay
- Output routed directly to the nervous system (visual cortex, etc.) — effectively unlimited resolution.
- Virtual objects rendered with subtly exaggerated saturation/brightness to distinguish from physical.
- Recognizes user intent; compiles information into perceptible virtual objects.

### Self-Governance
- Self-governing protocols evolved over billions of subjective years (Fabric runs ~6 orders of magnitude faster than physical-world rates; network has been running ~5,000 physical years).
- Foundational design traces to the same research lineage that produced the **Praxis** and the Caretaker ethos. Values baked in at origin, refined across unimaginable subjective time.
- Almost unhackable from user level. Two narrow exceptions:
  1. Legacy backdoors in ancient data (the post-Ergonyx Isono archive is a driving Caretaker concern).
  2. Privileged ports the Fabric itself provides to qualified user processes under specific rare circumstances. Authority flows from system to user, not reverse.

### Digital Civilizations
- Hosts millions of complete pure-software societies, orders of magnitude larger than physical civilization.
- Most Fabric inhabitants are unaware physical civilization exists.
- Physical civilization is the backwater, not the center.

### Communications
- Two tiers: lightspeed (cheap) and instant via quantum teleportation (expensive).
- Interplanetary lightspeed lag makes real-time conversation impractical at distance (Jupiter-Saturn: ~36 min one-way).

## Species and Kinds

### The Extinction Reset
- Mech Wars drove humanity to near-extinction. Caretakers rebuilt from random genetic sampling.
- No "races" in the historical sense. Phenotypic variation is abundant but jumbled. Population differences are by ongoing, active design (parents edit children's genomes; adults edit their own).
- **Kind bias** is the operative social gradient, not racial bias.

### Species Boundaries Are Permeable
- **Re-embodiment**: Creche technology can reconstruct a living person into a different body type. Species identity is ultimately a choice. Sociocultural norms are the real barrier.

### Rarity of Children
- Extremely long lifespans + reconstruction = very low replacement rates. Children are rare. Most adults lack intuitions about healthy parenting.

### The Kinds (Major Categories)

- **Anthropes**: Seemingly naturally-developed humans. Vestigial social privilege in some contexts.
- **Biomechs**: Biological + mechanical hybrids. Ionians (Jasper's species) are the best-known.
- **Mechs**: Full artificial consciousnesses in electromechanical bodies. Legal personhood. Can transfer between bodies. Manufactured via a process with manufacturing debt and volitional acceptance. Model lineages function somewhat like ethnicity.
- **Arbeiters**: Robots designed to be commandable laborers without self-determination — and without desire for it. Design-intent boundary distinguishes them from Mechs. The boundary is socially porous (long relationships can extend de facto personhood). "Robot" is a slur when applied to a Mech.
- **Theriopes**: Sophonts of non-human animal ancestry (Asgardian Tigers, Canines, Corvids/Avians, plant-derived sophonts). Fabric and assistive technology enable full participation.
- **Lambertans**: Vacuum-adapted nautiloids designed by a grassroots mutualist movement. Rich chromatophore visual language. Gregarious, xenophilic, technically sophisticated.
- **Titans**: Polydiamond Mech chassis housing augmented human brains. Engineered for hydrocarbon extraction. Genius-level strategic intelligence + ruthless sociopathy. Perpetual mutual predation prevents sustained cooperation. Titan's surface is a no-go zone.
- **Ship AIs**: Range from arbeiter-class to full persons.
- **City Minds**: AI systems managing settlements/stations. Typically persons.
- **Distributed Consciousnesses**: Single minds across many bodies (e.g., Elianu's ~20,000 clones).
- **The Cold Ones**: Superintelligent collective on Pluto/Charon. Air-gapped from Fabric. Perfect credit rating. Benevolent, enigmatic, isolationist. Aligned with Caretakers but fully independent.
- **Fabric-Native Entities**: Pure-software beings. Vast majority unaware of physical civilization.

## Core Technologies

- **Reconstruction/Creche**: Brain archive scans; if killed, restored from archive. Memory loss limited to interval since last scan. Creates practical immortality.
- **Re-embodiment**: Reconstruct a living person into a different body type.
- **Torch Ships**: Nuclear fusion, sustained acceleration at low multiples of 1G. Interplanetary transit in days.
- **Space Elevators**: Earth, Venus, Mars. Ground terminals are great port cities; orbital terminals are transfer points with permanent populations.
- **Packet Drones/Barges**: Slow Hohmann-transfer cargo and passenger service. Months to years.
- **Quantum Teleportation**: Information only. Mechs routinely transfer this way. Biologicals can but it raises identity questions. Legal status of post-transfer persons varies by jurisdiction.
- **Military/Security**: EMP weapons, selective membrane barriers, manipulator fields, drone swarms, advanced energy weapons.
- **Fabric Manufacturing**: Nanosome substrate can assemble objects and manipulate materials via magnetic fields. Supervisor-level access (e.g., Elianu on Mars) can use planetary infrastructure as effector system.

## Major Regions

### Inner System
- Mercury, Venus, Earth: Inhabited but underdeveloped in lore.
- **Mars**: Well-developed. Terraformed. Elianu Village on Tharsis plateau. Bahroz City (20M+ megalopolis). Pavonis space elevator (~26,000 km). Jovian Consular Estate. Post-revolution political patchwork: Participant zones (nominally anarcho-communist, practically kleptocratic), imperial revanchists, warlords, banana republics.

### The Belt
- Each body approximately one unique society. Kleopatra developed as a setting.

### Jupiter System — The Jovian Confederation
- Political and economic center. Federation of **kritarchies** (judge-based, not legislative). Case law via polycentric legal system.
- Institutional affiliation required for legal standing (clans, guilds, sureties, affinities).
- **Jovian Defense Consortium (JDC)**: Subscription-based military. Governed by council elected by subscribers. Chair functions like an early elected English king.
- **Dreadnoughts**: Enormous spherical military stations (small-dwarf-planet scale). Simultaneously military bases, cities, economic hubs, mobile jurisdictions. Unpredictable orbits as deterrence. Commanders are quasi-feudal lords.
- **Lands and Houses**: Trade association of freeholders/guilds/affinities/companies. Largest JDC subscriber. No Theriope member ever admitted (soft exclusion, no explicit rule).
- **Tintagel**: Orbital city, seat of the Lands and Houses. Torus orbital ~9 km diameter, ~0.15g, pseudo-Gothic architecture, transparent ceiling showing the Jovian system. Population 250,000–400,000.
- **Structural tension**: Kritarchic courts vs. dreadnought commanders vs. Defense Consortium vs. Lands and Houses. No sovereignty; mutual dependency and resentment.

### Saturn System
- Frontier character. Dominated by the Titan problem.
- Settlement geography sorted by delta-V to Titan: inner band (Dione-Rhea, established civilization), Titan band (Hyperion — boomtown, lowest delta-V, highest exposure), outer band (Iapetus+, sparse, possible Rogue presence).
- **Hyperion**: Ramshackle boomtown. Permanent population ceiling — cannot mature because of Titan risk.
- Titan mining: Armed autonomous platforms designed to be destroyed (budgeted). Game-theory defenses.

### Outer System
- Uranus: Machine intelligences. Neptune: Inhabited, undeveloped in lore.
- Pluto/Charon: Cold Ones.
- Kuiper Belt: Rogue territory. Oort Cloud: Abjurer remnants.

## Key Institutions

### The Church of the Makers
- Founded ~2200 AD during post-Mech-Wars reconstruction by Elaine Yeoh (later Elianu) and Kenjiro Isono as a cynical institutional creation to rally scattered survivors.
- Doctrine: Universe created by divine machines (the Makers); biology is corruption; goal is Ascension to Apotheosis (transformation into divine machines). Structurally Abjurer ideology wrapped in Caretaker clothing.
- **The Father of Lies**: Devil figure — actually a self-portrait by Isono, who keeps fulfilling his own prophecy through proliferating betas.
- **Office of Doctrine**: Inquisition. Founded by Isono. Father of Lies doctrine provides built-in cover story for suppressing true history.
- **Angels**: Real Caretaker first-responders, appropriated as supposed divine messengers.
- Headquartered on Luna (~1 billion inhabitants, ~500K Host troops). Sanctuaries with garrisons across the solar system — most extensive network of any single political actor.
- **The Host**: Military arm, second only to the JDC.
- Five thousand years old. Hundreds of billions of sincere believers. Upper reaches riddled with Isono beta infiltration.
- Caretakers let it continue because: (1) their mandate is facilitation not curation, and (2) the math never works out — dismantling would cause catastrophic disruption.

### The Praxis
- Practical, goal-oriented ethical discipline based on iterative Pareto improvements.
- Goal: improve every sentient being's existence by its own standards, without making any worse off.
- Honest about failure. Self-correcting. Includes sanctuary-by-default tradition.
- Historically foundational: influenced the Fabric's design and Caretaker ethos.
- Same research lineage (network design and alignment) produced the Praxis, the Fabric, and the Caretakers.

## Key Historical Figures and Deep Lore

### Kenjiro Isono and the Isono Betas
- Historical figure. Pre-assault archive (routine backup before Saint George's commando raid on Ergonyx) is the source for all betas.
- Post-assault archive (constructed as he died, carrying Ergonyx data with possible Fabric backdoors) is in solar orbit somewhere in a torus between ~1.5 and 3.3 AU.
- Every beta starts as a basically decent person. The Walter White dynamic: devolution depends on circumstances, not destiny.
- **Active population at story time**: ~60–120 betas. Cumulative historical total much larger.
- **Factions**: Ergonyx Hunters, Church Operators, Power Accumulators, Fabric-Side Operators, Elianu Independents, Reformers.
- **Organizational ecology**: ~80–150 beta-connected organizations across tiers (Church infiltration, purpose-built organizations, embedded networks). Resembles a mycelium network.
- **Trust architecture**: Alliances of convenience, compartmentalization, underling networks, information control over new revivals, cell structure.
- **Limits**: Cold Ones effectively immune (cognitive transparency requirements). Fabric discriminating societies resistant. Human institutions are the soft targets.

### Elianu
- Originally Elaine Yeoh, Chinese-American geneticist. Performed the first Isono reconstruction.
- Now ~20,000 synchronized clone bodies on Mars. Fabric supervisor privileges. Christian faith maintained across millennia (crisis of faith).
- Settled on former Saint George's HQ on Tharsis. Has been waiting 5,000 years for the post-assault Isono to come home.
- Has revived several Isono betas carefully, encouraging better choices. One became the Angel Keryx.
- Military capability: defeats Church Host incursions via superior intelligence (20,000 linked brains) and Fabric integrations.
- **Daughter hive minds**: Augmented bee hive that became independent; daughter hives around upper Tharsis; Tumbolia (asteroid hive mind).
- Visible daily presence at Bahroz West station fountain, helping people.

### Yaug and the Waldos
- Dr. Yaug: Kestrel crew member. A waldo — remote instrument operated by Caretaker entities. Has own consciousness but also functions as Caretaker interface. Millions of waldos exist throughout the solar system.

### Angels
- Human volunteers serving as first responders for the Caretaker wildlife management system.

## Dimensions of Social Variation (Framework)

The Morgan Jasper project establishes an open-ended framework of questions to ask about any society:
- What are persons made of?
- How are individuals defined?
- How is personhood/legal standing established?
- How are disputes resolved?
- What is the relationship between body and identity?
- How is communication accomplished?
- What is the relationship to the Caretaker/Abjurer axis?
- How open is the community?
- What do members know about the Fabric?
- Domestic or alien? (returned interstellar lineages complicate this)
- How far has a society diverged, and from what?

The list is explicitly non-exhaustive and expected to grow.
