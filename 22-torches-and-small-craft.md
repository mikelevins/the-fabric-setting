# Torches and Small Craft: Maneuver

**Source**: mikel, 2026-09-23 (Book 4 sitting; ruled while deriving how an attacker approaches *Kestrel* in Mars orbit). Ruled canon; derived items marked.
**Scope**: system-wide.

---

**The advantages and disadvantages rhyme with ocean-going vessels.** A torch ship makes far more thrust than any orbital craft and can reach much higher top speeds — speeds small craft cannot approach. *(Corrected 9/24, mikel: an earlier wording said a torch accelerates at higher rates than orbital craft, which is wrong. A torch's advantage is thrust and sustained delta-v, not acceleration: a small craft can out-accelerate a massive torch in short bursts — e.g. Khofir's shuttle at 1 g against a torch's ~0.2 g on a week's Belt-to-Mars run.)* Like a large ocean-going ship it is massive: **it cannot make quick short-range trajectory adjustments; it needs a lot of room and time to maneuver and accelerate.** Small orbiters are like small ocean craft: they cannot reach a torch's speeds, but **they can run rings around a big ship as it comes in to dock.**

**Consequence:** to escape an attacker, a torch has to know it is an attacker **before** the torch is on a rendezvous trajectory with its orbit. **Once a torch has begun its insertion into orbit, it cannot escape smaller, quicker craft.** *(Derived: the insertion and settling window is therefore the one time a torch in company with small craft is a fixed target; a forewarned crew can see the approach and cannot evade it, only prepare for what arrives.)*
