# Torches and Small Craft: Maneuver

**Source**: mikel, 2026-09-23 (Book 4 sitting; ruled while deriving how an attacker approaches *Kestrel* in Mars orbit). Ruled canon; derived items marked.
**Scope**: system-wide.

---

**The advantages and disadvantages rhyme with ocean-going vessels.** A torch ship can accelerate at much higher rates than any orbital craft and reach speeds small craft cannot. Like a large ocean-going ship it is massive: **it cannot make quick short-range trajectory adjustments; it needs a lot of room and time to maneuver and accelerate.** Small orbiters are like small ocean craft: they cannot reach a torch's speeds, but **they can run rings around a big ship as it comes in to dock.**

**Consequence:** to escape an attacker, a torch has to know it is an attacker **before** the torch is on a rendezvous trajectory with its orbit. **Once a torch has begun its insertion into orbit, it cannot escape smaller, quicker craft.** *(Derived: the insertion and settling window is therefore the one time a torch in company with small craft is a fixed target; a forewarned crew can see the approach and cannot evade it, only prepare for what arrives.)*
